## Description

Split or stage the current broad document processing flow so OCR, translation, translated artifacts, embeddings, and future intelligence jobs can be replayed independently without unnecessary full reprocessing.

## Acceptance

- The design preserves current v1 indexing behavior and lease-token ownership semantics.
- At least one concrete stage can be replayed independently with persisted status/timeline evidence.
- Job kinds, handler registration, retry behavior, and failure semantics are documented and tested.
- OpenAPI and SDK are updated if job or reprocess contracts change.
