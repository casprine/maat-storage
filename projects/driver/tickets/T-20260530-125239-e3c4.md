## Description

Add lease ownership or completion-token semantics so a worker/admin cannot complete or fail an arbitrary running job by guessing or knowing the job ID.

## Acceptance

- Job lease responses include an ownership token or equivalent identity material that is not exposed in document detail/list responses.
- Complete and fail endpoints reject missing, wrong, expired, or stale lease ownership.
- Existing admin/worker auth behavior remains protected and documented in OpenAPI if contract fields change.
- Tests cover valid completion, wrong token, stale token, and non-running job conflicts.
