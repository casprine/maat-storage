## Description

Leases were a flat 30 minutes with no renewal, so long jobs could be double-leased and crashed workers held jobs for 30 minutes. Add POST /jobs/{job-id}/heartbeat, make the worker renew while processing, and drop the default lease to 5 minutes.

## Acceptance

- Renewal, wrong token 409, expired lease cannot be renewed, finished job 409
- Worker keeps a long job alive and abandons it on lease loss
- Crashed worker's job is re-leased after the lease lapses
