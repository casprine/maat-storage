## Description

Ensure a document still awaiting indexing does not remain failed because its worker job was leased with a source path that is moved before reconciliation completes.

## Acceptance

- A move preserves indexed documents without a new job, but returns a non-indexed document with a stale running, retrying, or failed job to a runnable queued state at its new source path.
- Targeted tests cover the stale-job recovery path and do not alter indexed-document move behavior.
